What happens when I open a blocked app?

During your detox, there is no ignore button available, so you will not be able to escape when you feel like it. It may feel hard, but that is your brain recalibrating to its natural levels.

No instant unlock. There is no one tap way back in, anywhere in the app. If you want room to breathe, you decide that in advance, while you are calm.

Replace scrolling easily. Write in your journal, log a habit, read a book, or talk to Echo. All four are already inside Offliner, one tap away, so you are not sent into another app that also scrolls.

Lock Mode is the thing that stops you negotiating with yourself.

Lock Mode is on by default. While it is on you cannot edit or delete a rule inside its active window, and you cannot pull an app out of a detox early.

If you turn it off, it only takes effect the next day, so the decision is never made in the middle of a craving.

What does Offliner include?

01

App blocking

The default block is a detox. Add an app to a detox and it is completely blocked for the time you select. When the detox ends, the app graduates, and you can set the rules for each app individually (a daily time limit, a countdown before it opens, a friend must give you access, and more).

You can also create rules for any app without going through a detox first, but we recommend doing at least one detox to restore your dopamine levels, which makes it easier in the long term.

02

Streaks and XP

You earn one XP for every minute you are offline. There are 1,440 minutes in a day, so a day fully offline is worth 1,440 XP. A higher streak increases the rate at which you earn XP, and every five levels you unlock something new inside the app.

1,440 XP in a fully
offline day
03

Focus sessions

Pomodoro timer with breaks, or just a normal timer. You can select the apps to block during these sessions, so they are not able to open.

04

Insights

Screen time analytics with a built-in algorithm. It notices compulsive patterns and offers you suggestions. "10pm is when you open TikTok the most. Want a night schedule?" One tap and it is a rule.

05

Echo, journal, and books

Echo is an AI support buddy for your screen time journey. The journal helps you take notes and work through it. Books are gifted to you for reaching certain levels through XP and your streak.
