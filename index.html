How it works

Three things, and then you get out of your own way.

1

Pick what is taking your time

Detox from an app completely for up to 30 days, or leave it open with a daily limit you set yourself. Your limits, your caliber. Not somebody else's idea of what you should manage.

2

Lock Mode keeps you honest

It is on by default. While it is on you cannot quietly loosen your own rules the second you crave. Turn it off and it only takes effect tomorrow, when you are calm again.

3

Every clean day builds the streak

Stay under your limits and the day counts. Your streak also raises your XP multiplier, so the longer you hold it, the more every offline minute is worth to you.

The moment it matters

The screen you get when you reach for it anyway.

Every other blocker hands you a padlock and an Ignore button, which takes about a second to tap. Offliner does something else with that second.

  • No instant unlock. There is no one tap way back in, anywhere in the app. If you want room to breathe, you decide that in advance, while you are calm.
  • Somewhere else to go. Write in your journal, log a habit, read a book, or talk to Echo. All four are already inside Offliner, one tap away, so you are not sent into another app that also scrolls.
  • Your numbers, not a lecture. What this app has cost you today, and what your streak is currently worth.

Inside the app

Built so that staying off is the thing that pays.

Nothing in Offliner rewards you for using Offliner. It only ever rewards you for the time you did not spend scrolling.

Your streak, and your level

You earn one point for every minute you are not in a distracting app. There are 1,440 minutes in a day, so a fully offline day is worth 1,440 of them. Every five levels unlocks something new inside the app, and your streak multiplies everything you earn.

Detox, then limits

Add an app to a detox and it is gone completely, for anywhere up to 30 days. You can add more apps to a running detox at any time. When it ends the app graduates to a daily limit instead of a block, so you get it back on your terms rather than all at once.

Focus sessions

A timer, or a pomodoro with breaks you can safely stop in. While it runs, the apps you picked simply are not reachable.

Insights that suggest

It notices your pattern and offers you the fix. "10pm is when you reach for TikTok the most. Want a night schedule?" One tap and it is a rule.

Echo, the journal, the books

Echo is there at 3am when the craving is. The journal is where you work out what set it off. The books are what you read instead.

Common questions

What Offliner is, in plain terms.

What is the Offliner app?

Offliner is a screen time and app blocking app for iPhone and iPad. You choose which apps to detox from and which to limit, Offliner enforces it, and every day you stay under your limits counts towards a streak. It was built by Rodrigo Atiye and is free to download on the App Store.

How is it different from Apple Screen Time?

Screen Time puts an "Ignore Limit" button on every block screen, and that takes about a second to tap. Offliner has no instant unlock. On top of that you get a streak worth protecting, XP for every minute you stay offline, and somewhere to go the moment you close the app you were about to open.

What is Lock Mode?

It is the thing that stops you negotiating with yourself. Lock Mode is on by default. While it is on you cannot edit or delete a rule inside its active window, and you cannot pull an app out of a detox early. If you turn it off, it only takes effect the next day, so the decision is never made in the middle of a craving.

How do levels and XP work?

You earn one point for every minute you are not in a distracting app. A day is 1,440 minutes, so a fully offline day is worth 1,440 points before your streak multiplier. Nothing else pays, including opening Offliner itself. Higher levels take more, and every five levels unlocks something new inside the app.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

Free to download, with in app purchases for the full feature set. No sign up and no account. Everything is worked out on your phone and stays there, and Apple hides app names from developers, so nobody here can see which apps you picked.
